Kit Hangar Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Summary: Kit Hangar does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What you enter

Your kits, wishlist items, price notes, tool checklist and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. The developer never receives them.

Device backups

If your phone's backup service is turned on (for example, Google's), it may include the app's data. Those backups are handled by the backup provider under its own terms.

Backup files you export

Export Backup creates a file on your device and opens your share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Links

Opening a product link you saved loads that website in a browser. The website's own privacy policy applies there.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Deleting your data

Use Erase All Data in the app's Insights & Backups screen, or uninstall the app. Because no data is collected, there is nothing for the developer to delete on a server.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
